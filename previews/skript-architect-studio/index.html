How a project runs

Five steps from the first site visit to the day you move in.

  1. Step 1

    Consultation and site visit

    We listen to what you need, measure every room and photograph the site.

    You get: a measured plan

  2. Step 2

    Design and 3D

    Layout, mood board and 3D views. Changes happen on screen, where they cost nothing.

    You get: approved designs

  3. Step 3

    Quote and materials

    An itemised quote with every item and its price, and materials picked with you from real samples.

    You get: a fixed quote

  4. Step 4

    Work on site

    Carpentry, ceilings, electrical and painting, with weekly photo updates on WhatsApp.

    You get: progress updates

  5. Step 5

    Handover

    A final walkthrough, every small defect fixed, cleaning done and warranty papers handed over.

    You get: a ready home
