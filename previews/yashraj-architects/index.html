How a project runs

Four steps from the first site visit to the day you move in.

  1. 1

    Site visit and measuring

    We visit the site, take exact measurements and listen to how you want to use every room.

  2. 2

    Layout and 3D design

    You see the floor plan and 3D views before anything is built, so changes happen on paper, where they cost nothing.

  3. 3

    Materials and work on site

    Finishes are chosen with you from real samples, and the work on site follows the approved drawings.

  4. 4

    Handover

    A final walk-through together, every last fix closed, and the space handed back ready to live in.
