How your furniture gets made

Four steps from the first visit to the day it is ready.

  1. Step 1

    Measurement at your home

    We measure the kitchen or room at your home, so every unit fits the wall it is made for.

  2. Step 2

    Design and finish

    Choose the layout, shutters and colours with us. You see the design before anything is made.

  3. Step 3

    Made to size

    Each unit is made in uPVC to your measurements: it won't swell with water and termites leave it alone.

  4. Step 4

    Fitting at home

    Our team fits everything at your home and checks every door, drawer and hinge before leaving.
