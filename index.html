(04) Process

How a project runs

  1. i.

    Talk

    We talk about your business, your customers and what the site needs to do.

  2. ii.

    Design

    I design the key screens and share a live link you can open on your phone.

  3. iii.

    Build

    I build the full site — WhatsApp, maps, forms — and refine it with your feedback.

  4. iv.

    Launch

    It goes live on your own domain, and I check that every link and button works.

(05) About

The name means truth

satyam सत्यम्

Sanskrit · noun

Truth; that which is real.

I’m Satyam Choure, an independent web designer and developer from Madhya Pradesh, India. I build websites the way I’d want my own business’s website built — from a blank page, around what customers actually come to do: see the prices, check the timings, call, book or order.

I take my name as a brief. Real content over filler. Honest claims over inflated ones. Sites that do exactly what they say.

When I’m not building for clients, I’m building my own products — starting with Flexippo.
