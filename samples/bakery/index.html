      Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

      Eggless cakes aur mithai milte hain?
      Haan bilkul! Hamari zyada-tar mithai pehle se eggless hai, aur har cake flavour eggless option mein available hai (thoda extra charge lagta hai — ₹50/kg [SAMPLE]). Order ke waqt “Eggless” zaroor likhein, hum dhyan rakhenge.
      Kitne ghante/din pehle order karna padta hai?
      Normal mithai aur bakery ke liye same-day order chalega. Custom cakes ke liye kam se kam 24 hours [PLACEHOLDER] pehle order karein. Wedding, party aur bulk orders ke liye 3–4 din ka time dijiye taaki sab fresh aur perfect bane.
      Kahan-kahan delivery hoti hai?
      Bhopal ke andar ~8 km tak home delivery available hai — Kolar Road, MP Nagar, Arera Colony, Bairagarh, Shahpura aur aas-paas ke areas. ₹500+ order par delivery free hai. [PLACEHOLDER: delivery area & charges confirm karein] Pickup bhi kar sakte hain — dukaan ka address neeche hai.
      Payment kaise karein?
      Cash, UPI (PhonePe / Google Pay / Paytm), aur debit/credit cards — sab chalta hai. Bulk orders ke liye advance payment leni padti hai (50% booking amount). WhatsApp par payment link/details bhej di jaayegi.
      Mithai kitne din fresh rehti hai?
      Fresh mithai (Rasmalai, Gulab Jamun, milk-based) 2–3 din tak refrigerator mein best rehti hai. Dry mithai (Kaju Katli, Soan Papdi, ladoo) 7–10 din chalti hai — airtight dabbe mein rakhein. Har box par best-before likha hota hai.
