    08 — Questions

    Questions, answered plainly.

    Do you travel outside Bhopal?

    Yes. Within 25 km of MP Nagar travel is included. Beyond that, we quote travel on WhatsApp. [PLACEHOLDER policy]

    How do I hold my date?

    A 30% advance secures your slot. Pay by UPI or bank transfer, and we send a written confirmation. [PLACEHOLDER]

    What if I need to cancel or move?

    Cancel more than 7 days ahead and your advance is refunded minus ₹500 processing. Inside 7 days the advance is kept, but you can move the date once. [PLACEHOLDER policy]

    When will I get my photos?

    Portraits, family and newborn: edited gallery within 7 days. Weddings: within 21 days. The 48-hour express edit is available as an add-on. [PLACEHOLDER]

    Do you photograph babies safely?

    Yes. Newborn sessions are parent-led. We never pose a baby in a way that feels unsafe, and we stop the moment baby needs a break.

    Will you use my photos publicly?

    Only with your written permission. We ask before any portfolio or social use, and you can opt out at any time.
