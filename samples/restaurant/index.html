05 — Questions

Quick answers, no fuss.

Do you deliver, and how far? [SAMPLE]

Yes. We deliver within 5 km of MP Nagar on orders over ₹400. Delivery is usually 30–40 minutes.

Can I get my order without a call?

Yes. Build your order here, tap Send on WhatsApp, and we confirm by message. Nothing is charged online.

Are there vegetarian and jain options?

Yes, almost half the menu is vegetarian. Jain dishes are cooked without onion or garlic on request [SAMPLE].

What is your refund policy?

If a dish is wrong or cold, we re-cook it free. If we cannot fix it, we refund the dish in cash or by UPI [SAMPLE].

How do you handle allergens?

Tell us in the notes field when you book or order. We flag nuts, dairy and gluten in our kitchen, but cannot guarantee a nut-free environment.
