Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to pay advance online to book?

No online gateway fees! Send your dates on WhatsApp and our reception desk confirms availability immediately. You can pay at check-in or send a small UPI token to hold late-night arrivals.

Are unmarried couples or families with kids welcome?

Families, business travellers, and guests with valid government photo ID (18+) for every adult are welcome.

Is safe parking available for cars and two-wheelers?

Yes, we have gated, CCTV-monitored parking right inside the hotel compound at no extra cost.
